# Shadow Ops Portfolio

## Overview

This portfolio website serves as the professional digital presence of **Saravana Priyan**, showcasing expertise in Cyber Security, Security Operations, Network Security, Digital Forensics, and Software Development.

The website highlights technical skills, certifications, projects, professional experience, achievements, and community involvement. It is designed to provide recruiters, collaborators, and industry professionals with a comprehensive view of technical capabilities, hands-on experience, and ongoing contributions to the cybersecurity ecosystem.

**Live Website:** https://shadow-ops-portfolio.vercel.app/

---

## Purpose

The primary objective of this portfolio is to:

* Present professional and academic achievements.
* Showcase cybersecurity and software development projects.
* Highlight certifications, technical skills, and areas of expertise.
* Demonstrate practical experience gained through internships, leadership roles, workshops, and community activities.
* Provide an accessible platform for networking and professional opportunities.
